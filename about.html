E-Prime Mail

Temporary email and Gmail aliases for Android

E-Prime Mail is an Android app that lets you create disposable email addresses and read the messages sent to them, so you can keep your real inbox private when signing up for websites and services.

What the app does

  • Creates temporary inboxes using the mail.tm and Guerrilla Mail services.
  • Optionally connects to your Google account to create Gmail aliases (a "+" alias such as name+tag@gmail.com and a dotted alias) and shows only the messages sent to the alias you are using.
  • Lets you open and delete (move to Trash) those messages.

Google account access

If you choose to connect Google, the app asks for permission to read and manage your Gmail messages. It uses this permission only to show messages sent to your alias addresses and to move them to Trash when you ask. Everything happens on your own device. The developer does not receive, store or see your emails.

E-Prime Mail's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
