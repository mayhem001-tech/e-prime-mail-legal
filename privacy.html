Privacy Policy

E-Prime Mail · Last updated: October 5, 2026

This policy explains how the E-Prime Mail Android app ("the app") handles information. Back to home page

1. Summary

E-Prime Mail runs entirely on your device. The developer operates no server for the app and does not collect, receive, store, sell or share your personal data or your emails.

2. Information the app handles

  • Temporary inboxes (mail.tm and Guerrilla Mail): the app sends the temporary address you create, and requests for its messages, directly from your device to those third-party services. The messages you receive are shown in the app. Their handling is governed by their own privacy policies.
  • Google account (optional): if you tap "Connect Google", the app requests access to your Gmail through Google's authorization screen. Your Google email address and the alias addresses you generate are stored only in the app's local storage on your device.
  • Gmail messages: the app reads the messages sent to the alias you are using, shows them to you, and moves a message to Trash only when you choose to delete it. Message content is displayed in the app and is not copied to any developer-owned server.

3. Google user data and Limited Use

The app requests the Gmail scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.modify solely to provide its alias inbox feature: listing and reading messages addressed to your alias, and moving messages to Trash on your request.

E-Prime Mail's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Specifically:

  • Google user data is used only to provide the user-facing features described above.
  • It is not transferred or sold to third parties, and it is not used for advertising.
  • It is not read by humans, because it never leaves your device, except where you share a message yourself.

4. Data storage and retention

Settings, your connected Google email address and generated aliases are kept in the app's private storage on your device. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app removes them. Temporary inbox credentials are also stored locally so you can reopen your inbox.

5. Revoking access

You can disconnect E-Prime Mail from your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. You can also delete the app's data from your device settings.

6. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
